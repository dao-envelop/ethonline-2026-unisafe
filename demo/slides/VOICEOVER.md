# Voice-over for the end cards

Two slides, 7 seconds each. Confident narration runs **145–160 words per minute**, so a 7-second line
is **17–20 words** — everything below is counted. Read the **tight** version if you want air at the end
of the shot; read the **full** version only if you can hold the slide for 8 seconds.

Say "move liquidity" and "suggest move" as words. Never read a slide aloud verbatim — the viewer is
already reading it; the voice should say the thing the slide does not.

---

## Slide 1 — the concept

**Tight — 19 words**

> The Graph indexes every position. The agent proposes a move, Chainlink checks it, and only the NFT
> holder can withdraw.

**Full — 23 words, needs 8 seconds**

> The Graph indexes every position. The agent proposes a move between Uniswap pools, Chainlink checks it,
> and only the NFT holder can withdraw.

Name the sponsors in the order the slide reads, left to right — the voice then walks the diagram
instead of competing with it. The tight version drops Uniswap, not The Graph: the pools are the one box
a viewer can identify without being told.

Pause after "proposes a move" — that is where the eye reaches the vault. Land on "withdraw": it is the
whole claim, and the slide's last line repeats it.

---

## Slide 2 — before and during

**Tight — 19 words**

> Left of the line existed before. In nine days: the cross-pool move, the guard on every action, the index.

**Full — 21 words, needs 8 seconds**

> Left of the line existed before. In nine days: Uniswap's cross-pool move, Chainlink on every action, The
> Graph behind the agent.

Prefer the full version here if the timing allows — naming the three sponsors out loud is the point of
this slide, and the tags on screen confirm rather than carry it. Pause after "existed before"; the three
items then read as a list, one per beat.

---

## If you would rather not narrate over slide 2

Hold slide 1 with voice and let slide 2 run silent for 7 seconds. Five tagged lines are more than a
viewer can read and listen to at once, and the tags do the work without help.
